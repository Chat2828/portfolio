-North-

North was a character I designed purely out of self indulgence in my favorite design tropes. Thanks to this, I was actually able to use him as a learning base for a variety of digital art techniques and standard art practice; ie posing, shading, coloring, etc.

-Inky-

Inky is a standalone character with the backstory of being a water-borne parasite that infects humans and slowly dissolves and consumes the hosts' innards. The art shows Inky in their current human skinsuit, with the addition of several zippers for ease of removal.

-EAHK-

EAHK- short for Emperor And His King- is a longstanding creative writing project of mine that I have worked on shockingly little for the amount I love the premise. I will go more into detail on it (and the shown characters) in my project page.

-Piet Fairchild-

Originally, Piet was a modern reinterpretation of Peter Pan. Though he quickly spiraled into his own character, he still draws plenty of inspiration from his source material.

-Forest Guardians-

I haven't put much thought into this pair, but their names are Chatwyn and Leven, and are mysterious entities guarding some form of the Feywilde.

-Lennie-

Lennie is a character I designed to be a supervillain for a storyline that unfortunately flopped. However, his concept was strong enough that I haven't let go of him quite yet. As evidenced by the art, he is a very small human who can turn into a very large shark (with legs!).

-Helene-

Helene is the protagonist of a long-term project I've been stewing on for the past four or so years, and has gone through many iterations before coming to where she is now. I'll go over her and her lore more in depth in my projects page.

-Vanraj Khan-

Vanraj is my son. I hold him as a character very close to my heart, and I doubt I could fully explain his complexities in this paragraph. That aside, he is most concisely described as a character study on teenaged bullies and the "mean jock" archetype.
